## Stage 1: Purdue Campus Round — Boiler Up, Hammer Data Challenge

- **Deadline:** December 11, 2026 at 11:59 PM (confirm any updates on the [Purdue Love Data Week page](https://www.purdue.edu/idata/lovedata/)).
- **Eligibility:** Purdue students competing solo or in pairs (max 2 students; all contributors credited). Verify full eligibility rules on Purdue's site.
- **What You Build:**
  - Must use the official **NCAA Women's Basketball dataset** (from the [official Box folder](https://uofi.app.box.com/s/vhbl9evt5aoqyiduq4pkgehm5on9cuaq)) as the core dataset. Supplemental public data is permitted.
  - Any visualization tool or stack is allowed (e.g., Tableau Public, Power BI, Python/Streamlit, R Shiny, Observable/D3, or static web pages on GitHub Pages).
  - Must be a genuine data visualization rather than a purely decorative infographic.
- **What You Submit (via the [Microsoft Submission Form](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/r/WUdY0XHRFQ)):**
  - Project title, short description, and a **publicly viewable URL requiring no login** (check the form directly for all required fields and prompts).
- **Purdue Student Awards ($500 each):**
  - *Excellence in Business Insights* (Daniels School of Business) — best use of data to address a business question.
  - *Visionary Visualization* (Purdue Libraries & School of Information Studies) — most creative and well-designed visualization.
  - *Emerging Innovator* (School of Applied and Creative Computing) — applied technical skill and fresh approach (**undergraduate only**).

## Stage 2: Purdue Showcase — Boiler Up, Hammer Data Showcase

- **Date:** January 22, 2027 (check [Purdue's Love Data Week site](https://www.purdue.edu/idata/lovedata/) for current location, schedule, and format updates).
- **What to Expect:**
  - Entrants present their visualizations to fellow students, faculty, staff, and university leadership.
  - **Attendance Requirement:** At least one author must be available to present.
  - Campus award winners are announced, and judging/attendees select **one student submission** to represent Purdue at the Big Ten championship.

---

## Stage 3: Conference Round — BTAA Data Viz Championship (Love Data Week)

- **Dates:** February 8–12, 2027 (virtual conference-wide event across Big Ten Academic Alliance universities).
- **Official Details, Rubric & Current Rules:** <https://btaa.org/technology/love-data-week/data-viz-student-championship>
- **What Advancing Entails:**
  - Purdue's winning student entry competes virtually alongside representatives from other BTAA institutions.
  - A primary presenter is designated for the virtual showcase.
  - The submitted URL must remain publicly accessible with **no login** for conference-wide judging and audience voting.
- **BTAA Award Tracks & Judging Criteria:**
  - **Chart Topper (Judges' Award for Excellence):** Scored by a multi-institutional panel using the live rubric on the [BTAA championship page](https://btaa.org/technology/love-data-week/data-viz-student-championship) — covering *Visual Design & Presentation*, *Clarity of Communication*, *Accuracy & Ethical Data Representation* (avoiding truncated axes, misleading scales, or conflating correlation with causation), and *Effective Use of Data*.
  - **Love It! Award (People's Choice):** Decided by public audience votes during the virtual Love Data Week showcase.

## AI Usage & Disclosure

- **Policy:** AI tools are permitted under BTAA guidelines, subject to Purdue academic integrity policies. Always check the latest policy wording on the [Purdue](https://www.purdue.edu/idata/lovedata/) and [BTAA](https://btaa.org/technology/love-data-week/data-viz-student-championship) pages and the [Microsoft submission form](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/r/WUdY0XHRFQ).
- **Disclosure Practice:** BTAA collects disclosure on whether and how AI tools were used. Maintain a brief, accurate log of AI assistance as the project develops so submission disclosures are straightforward and complete.
